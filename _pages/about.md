My name is Alexander Tran, a social scientist, researcher, and data expert from Ontario, Canada. I am a trained social psychologist (PhD) with extensive experience conducting experiments (A/B testing) and using high level statistical models to understand human behaviour. My technical skills have crossed a number of disciplines; from behavioural research and survey design, processing/analysis of EEG signals, to population-level research (epidemiology). I use coding (MATLAB, R, Python, SQL, SAS) to support replicable and reliable statistical models (multivariate analysis, time series analyses, dimension-reduction, population segmented analyses). Professionally, I work as a data scientist at JD Power, have worked in the public sector (CAMH), taught at the post-secondary level and dabbled in entrepreneurship as a Behavioural Research Consultant. 

I apply my data wrangling, statistical modelling, visualization, and storytelling skills to any problem with curiousity and an attention-to-detail. I thrive in a fast-paced environment and rise to any challenge. I enjoy working with people, educating them on my techniques, and using data to inform decision-making.<br/><br/><br/><br/>
