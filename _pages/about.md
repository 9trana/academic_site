My name is Alexander Tran, a social scientist, researcher, and data-specialist from Ontario, Canada. I am a trained social psychologist (PhD) with extensive experience conducting experiments (A/B testing) aimed at understanding human behaviour. My technical and statistical modelling skills have crossed a number of disciplines; from behavioural research and survey design (HTML markup), processing/analysis of EEG signals, to population-level research (epidemiology). I use coding (MATLAB, R, Python, SQL) to support my desire to develop replicable and reliable statistical models (multivariate analysis, time series analyses, dimension-reduction, population segmented analyses). Professionally, I worked as a data scientist at CAMH, taught at the post-secondary level and dabbled in entrepreneurship as a Behavioural Research Consultant. 

I apply my data wrangling, statistical modelling, visualization, and storytelling skills to any problem with curiousity and an attention-to-detail. I thrive in a fast-paced environment and rise to any challenge. I enjoy working with people, educating them on my techniques, and using data to make predictions and inform decision-making.<br/><br/><br/><br/>
